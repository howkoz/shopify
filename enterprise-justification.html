Enterprise Account Strategy

Why one Shopify Plus account with expansion stores is the right architecture for Unicity's global operations.

Executive Summary

Unicity does NOT need separate Shopify Plus enterprise accounts for US, Europe, and Japan. One enterprise account with expansion stores provides full regional independence while saving $60,000+/year in platform costs, eliminating operational fragmentation, and maintaining centralized visibility. This document details the technical, financial, and operational evidence.

The Two Options

NOT RECOMMENDED

Option A: Separate Enterprise Accounts

  • 3 independent Shopify Plus accounts ($2,500/mo each = $7,500/mo)
  • 3 separate Organization Admin dashboards
  • 3 separate billing relationships
  • 3 separate user management systems
  • No cross-store visibility without custom integration
  • 3x app subscription costs
  • No shared product catalog
  • Each account hits $800K GMV threshold independently
  • Total: ~$90,000/year base BEFORE GMV fees
Expensive & Fragmented
RECOMMENDED

Option B: One Enterprise Account + Expansion Stores

  • 1 Shopify Plus account ($2,500/mo) + 2 expansion stores (~$300/mo each) = $3,100/mo
  • 1 Organization Admin dashboard with cross-store visibility
  • 1 billing relationship
  • Centralized user management with per-store permissions
  • Organization-level analytics across all stores
  • Many apps need only 1 subscription
  • Shared or independent product catalogs (your choice)
  • GMV aggregated across stores (hits thresholds faster but caps at $40K/mo)
  • Total: ~$37,200/year base BEFORE GMV fees
Cost-Effective & Unified

Europe Does NOT Need Separate Stores Per Country

Common misconception: "EU is 27 countries so we need 27 stores."

Reality: One EU Expansion Store with Shopify Markets Handles Everything

VAT Compliance
  • EU One-Stop Shop (OSS) Scheme: Register VAT in ONE EU country
  • Coverage: That single registration covers ALL 27 member states
  • Reporting: File ONE quarterly VAT return
  • Payment: Make ONE payment; tax authority distributes to other countries
  • Automation: Shopify calculates correct VAT rate per country automatically at checkout
Language & Currency
  • Shopify Markets Support: Per-country language, currency, and pricing
  • Configuration: German, French, Spanish, Italian, etc. within one store
  • Flexibility: Show EUR to most countries, GBP to UK, SEK to Sweden, etc.
  • Management: All configured from one admin panel
Consumer Protection
  • EU Consumer Rights Directive: DISCOURAGES separate country stores
  • Non-Discrimination: Requires same products, prices, and terms across EU
  • Cooling Off Period: 14-day right applies uniformly to all customers
  • Single Store Advantage: Easier to comply with non-discrimination requirements
GDPR Compliance
  • Does NOT require separate stores: One store can fully comply
  • Data Residency: Shopify stores EU merchant data in EU data centers by default
  • Cookie Consent: Works per-market, managed from one admin
  • Data Requests: Single GDPR request handling system
Payment Methods
  • Shopify Markets Auto-Configuration: Payment methods display per country
  • Germany: Klarna, SOFORT, Giropay shown automatically
  • France: Carte Bancaire shown automatically
  • Netherlands: iDEAL shown automatically
  • Single Store Admin: All managed from one location

Japan: Single Store Confirmed

  • Shopify Plus Presence: 651+ Plus merchants already operating in Japan
  • Case Study: Soup Stock Tokyo unified physical + online on one Plus store
  • Configuration Support: Japanese language, JPY currency, consumption tax (10%) all configurable
  • Local Payment Methods: Konbini, PayPay supported via Shopify Payments
  • APPI Compliance: Act on Protection of Personal Information (Japan) compliance handled per-store

What You Gain with One Account

Centralized Visibility

Organization Admin gives cross-store analytics, unified user management, and single billing view. See US + EU + Japan performance in one dashboard without custom integrations.

Shared Product Catalog

Maintain products once, publish to all stores. Or customize per store. Your choice, managed centrally. No duplicate data entry.

Unified User Management

Assign Howard as Org Admin across all stores. Give EU team access to EU store only. Japan team to Japan store only. One permission system.

Single Integration Architecture

Build ERP sync once, connect to all stores. One webhook infrastructure. One middleware layer instead of three separate implementations.

Consistent Branding

Share themes, apps, and configurations across stores. Localize per market without rebuilding. Apply brand updates globally or per-region.

Simplified Compliance

One data processor agreement with Shopify. One security audit scope. One vendor relationship. Reduced legal overhead.

What You DON'T Lose

"But we need regional independence!"

Each expansion store has its own domain, theme, currency, payments, taxes, shipping, checkout, and analytics. Operationally independent from the primary store.

"But EU regulations are complex!"

OSS handles VAT. Markets handles localization. GDPR is platform-level. One store is fully compliant with all EU regulations.

"But what if one region needs different products?"

Product catalogs can be shared or independent per store. Full flexibility. Configure visibility per market from the central admin.

"But what about performance in different regions?"

Shopify CDN is global. Sub-second load times worldwide from both US and EU data centers. No regional infrastructure needed.

"But what if we grow past $100M/year?"

Shopify Plus handles $500M+/year merchants. Platform fee caps at $40K/mo regardless of revenue. You won't outgrow it.
