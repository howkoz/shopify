Expansion Stores Guide

Managing multi-store architecture for US, European, and Japanese markets on Shopify Plus.

What Are Expansion Stores

▼

Shopify Plus includes powerful multi-store capabilities that enable brands to manage multiple independent storefronts under a single organization. Here's what you need to know:

Shopify Plus Multi-Store Breakdown:
  • 1 Primary Store: Included in Plus subscription ($2,500/mo)
  • Up to 9 Expansion Stores: Each adds ~$300/month to your bill
  • Independent Operations: Each store has its own domain, theme, currency, and payment gateways
  • Shared Organization: All stores share billing, user management, and organization-level settings

Key Characteristics

Aspect Details
Domain Each store gets its own custom domain (e.g., eu.unicity.com, jp.unicity.com)
Theme & Branding Independent theme configuration per store allows region-specific customization
Localization Language, currency, and regional content can be tailored to each market
Payment Methods Configure region-specific payment gateways and providers
Fulfillment Each store can have independent shipping zones, carriers, and fulfillment locations
Tax Configuration Region-specific tax rules, rates, and compliance settings

Shared vs Isolated Settings

▼

Understanding what's shared across your organization vs. what's isolated per store is critical for proper management:

Setting Type Scope Details
Billing SHARED Single organization bill for all stores. No per-store billing separation.
User Accounts SHARED Users exist at organization level. Permissions assigned per-store.
Apps & Subscriptions SHARED App licenses at org level. May need multiple subscriptions for multi-store use.
Organization Roles SHARED Admin, Editor, Viewer roles span all stores (unless limited at store level).
Theme ISOLATED Each store has independent theme. Can customize per region.
Domain ISOLATED Each store must have unique domain. Can be custom or myshopify.com.
Currency ISOLATED Set per store. Primary store currency is store's default.
Payment Methods ISOLATED Unique payment gateways per store. Region-specific providers supported.
Taxes ISOLATED Tax rules configured independently per store for each jurisdiction.
Shipping ISOLATED Shipping zones, rates, and carriers unique per store.
Checkout ISOLATED Language, policies, and settings per store.
Products ISOLATED Can be shared via Markets or kept separate. Inventory separate per store.
Customer Data ISOLATED Customers are store-specific. No cross-store view (unless using Markets).
Order Data ISOLATED Orders live in their respective stores. Separate fulfillment per store.
Analytics ISOLATED Store-level analytics viewable in Organization Admin for aggregated reporting.

Multi-Store User Management

▼

Managing users across multiple stores requires understanding both organization-level and store-level roles:

Organization-Level Roles

Role Permissions
Organization Admin Full access to all organization settings, user management, billing, and all stores
Organization Editor Can edit all stores but cannot manage organization settings or billing
Organization Viewer Read-only access to all stores. Can view reports and analytics across stores

Store-Level Roles

Role Scope Typical Use Case
Store Owner Full control of assigned store(s) Regional store manager
Full Access Complete store permissions Operations lead
Limited Access Restricted to specific areas (Products, Orders, etc.) Category managers, customer service
POS Only Access only to POS features Retail staff at physical locations

Regional Store Configuration Checklist

▼

Each region requires specific configurations tailored to local requirements:

US Store Configuration

Category Configuration
Currency USD (US Dollar)
Language(s) English (primary), Spanish (secondary supported)
Domain unicity-us.myshopify.com or custom unicity.com domain
Payment Methods Stripe (primary), WorldPay/Chase (alternative), Apple Pay, Google Pay
Tax Provider TaxJar or Avalara for automated sales tax by state
Shipping Carriers USPS, FedEx, UPS with live rate quoting
Tax Rate State-based sales tax (varies 0-8.6%). Automated via TaxJar
Fulfillment US-based warehouse. Multi-state shipping support
Compliance PCI-DSS, GDPR (if serving EU customers), CCPA compliance

EU Store Configuration

Category Configuration
Currency EUR (Euro primary), GBP (British Pound for UK)
Language(s) English, German, French, Spanish, Italian at minimum
Domain eu.unicity.com or unicity-eu.myshopify.com
Payment Methods Stripe EU (mandatory), PayPal, Klarna, Mollie, iDEAL, SEPA
Tax Provider Shopify Tax for automated VAT by country (27 EU + UK)
Shipping Carriers DHL, DPD, Royal Mail, Hermes, local carriers per country
Tax Rate VAT per country (15-27%). Automated per delivery location
Fulfillment EU warehouse or regional fulfillment centers. Multi-country shipping
Compliance GDPR (strict), PCI-DSS, VAT OSS, ETIAS, data residency requirements
EU Complexity: EU regulations are stringent. Ensure data is stored in EU servers, GDPR compliance is implemented, and VAT is correctly calculated per destination country. Consider legal review before launch.

Japan Store Configuration

Category Configuration
Currency JPY (Japanese Yen)
Language Japanese (primary), English optional
Domain jp.unicity.com or unicity-jp.myshopify.com
Payment Methods Stripe Japan, PayPay, Konbini (convenient store), Credit Cards, Bank Transfer
Tax Provider Shopify Tax for Japan consumption tax (Shouhizei)
Shipping Carriers Yamato (Kuroneko), Sagawa, Japan Post, local options
Tax Rate 10% consumption tax (standard), 8% reduced rate (food/beverages)
Fulfillment Japan-based warehouse. Same-day/next-day delivery common
Compliance APPI (Act on Protection of Personal Information), Local business registration, PCI-DSS
Japan Market Notes: Konbini (convenience store) payment is hugely popular. Customers pay at stores like 7-11, FamilyMart, Lawson. Strong preference for straightforward UX and fast delivery. Local logistics partners essential.

Cross-Store Operations

▼

Centralized Analytics & Reporting

The Organization Admin dashboard provides aggregated views across all stores:

  • Combined revenue and order metrics across regions
  • Customer growth trends by store
  • Comparative performance (US vs EU vs Japan)
  • Staff activity logs across organization

Shared Product Catalog Using Markets

Shopify Markets allows you to manage a single product catalog across stores with region-specific pricing and availability:

  • Create products once, publish to multiple markets
  • Override pricing per market/currency
  • Different product availability per region
  • Localized product descriptions and SEO

Inventory Visibility & Management

Aspect Details
Inventory per Store Each store maintains separate inventory counts. No automatic synchronization between stores.
Inventory Source Use Inventory Management feature to track multiple warehouse locations
Fulfillment Network Configure which warehouse fulfills orders for each store/region
Stock Planning Requires manual coordination. Use Shopify Reports or external tools for visibility.

Cross-Store Fulfillment

  • Regional Fulfillment: Best practice is regional fulfillment centers (US, EU, Japan) to minimize shipping costs and delivery time
  • Dropshipping: Can use single supplier for all stores via product fulfillment settings
  • 3PL Integration: Manage inventory across stores using 3PL platforms (e.g., ShipBob, Flexport)
  • No Automatic Transfers: Stock cannot automatically route between stores—requires manual or external integration

Customer Data & CRM

Important Limitation: Customers are store-specific. A customer who purchases on the US store is a separate customer record from the same person buying on the EU store. There is no native unified customer view across stores unless using a third-party CRM or Klaviyo-type integration.

Launch Sequence Recommendation

▼

A phased approach minimizes risk and allows learning from each launch:

Phase 1: US Store (Current / Q1 2026)

Duration: 2-3 months | Status: Primary Store
  • ✅ Primary store fully operational with Stripe + WorldPay
  • ✅ POS pilot in select US locations
  • ✅ USPS/FedEx/UPS shipping live
  • ✅ TaxJar integration for sales tax
  • ✅ Established ops team and processes

Phase 2: EU Expansion Store (Q2 2026)

Duration: 6-8 weeks total | Timeline: Setup (4 weeks) → Testing (2 weeks) → Launch (1 week)
  • 📋 Weeks 1-2: Create expansion store, configure domain (eu.unicity.com), set currency to EUR
  • 📋 Week 2: Integrate Stripe EU, configure VAT via Shopify Tax, add DHL/DPD carriers
  • 📋 Week 3: Launch with UK + Germany only (2 largest markets), expand to other countries after validation
  • 📋 Week 3-4: Translate theme and product descriptions to EN/DE/FR/ES
  • 📋 Week 4-5: Intensive testing of checkout, payments, tax calculations, shipping
  • 📋 Week 6: Parallel run (live + monitoring)
  • 📋 Week 7: Full EU launch

Phase 3: Japan Expansion Store (Q3 2026)

Duration: 6-8 weeks total | Timeline: Setup (4 weeks) → Testing (2 weeks) → Launch (1 week)
  • 📋 Weeks 1-2: Create expansion store, configure domain (jp.unicity.com), set currency to JPY
  • 📋 Week 2: Integrate Stripe Japan, add PayPay + Konbini payment apps, configure Yamato/Sagawa
  • 📋 Week 3: Full Japanese translation and localization (theme, products, support)
  • 📋 Week 3-4: Configure 10% consumption tax, business registration compliance (APPI)
  • 📋 Week 4-5: Rigorous testing of all payment methods, tax, and shipping
  • 📋 Week 6: Parallel run with small test group
  • 📋 Week 7: Full Japan launch

Post-Launch Activities

  • Monitor & Optimize: Track conversion rates, avg order value, payment failure rates per region
  • Customer Support: Ensure 24/7 regional support for each market
  • Inventory Coordination: Implement processes to coordinate stock across regional warehouses
  • Analytics Review: Weekly store performance reviews; monthly cross-store reporting
  • Compliance Audits: Quarterly reviews of GDPR (EU), APPI (Japan), and tax obligations
Best Practice: Do not launch all stores simultaneously. A phased approach allows your team to master each market sequentially and apply learnings to subsequent launches. Each phase should be fully stable before moving to the next.
