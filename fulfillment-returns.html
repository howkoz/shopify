📊 Shipping Strategy Options

Three fulfillment models offer different tradeoffs between speed, cost, and operational complexity:

Option A: Central Warehouse (US)

✓ Simplest operations

✓ Lowest initial capex

✓ Single inventory system

✗ Slow international delivery (7-14 days)

✗ Higher shipping costs (long-haul)

✗ Poor customer experience outside US

Best for: Launch phase, low volumes

Option B: Regional Warehouses

✓ Fast local delivery (2-5 days)

✓ Competitive shipping costs

✓ Best customer experience

✗ Higher inventory cost (duplication)

✗ Complex multi-location logistics

✗ Requires dedicated warehouse ops teams

Best for: High-growth, multiple markets

Option C: 3PL Partners

✓ Fully outsourced operations

✓ Scalable without capex

✓ 3PL handles inventory optimization

✗ Variable/high unit costs

✗ Less control over fulfillment

✗ Integration complexity

Best for: Scaling quickly, variable demand

Recommendation: Deploy Option A (central US warehouse) for EU/Japan launch. As order volume reaches 500+ units/month per region, transition to Option B regional warehouses. Option C 3PLs best used as overflow for peak seasons or supplemental capacity.

🚚 Carrier Selection by Market

📫USPS (United States Postal Service)

Best Value

Best for: Lightweight packages (<1 lb), domestic only, cost-sensitive orders

  • Speed: 2-7 day delivery (1st Class, Priority)
  • Cost: $2.50-8.50 per package (most competitive)
  • Features: Tracking, signature options, flat-rate boxes
  • Limitations: Weight limits, no international (except via USPS Global)
  • Integration: Shopify Shipping, EasyPost, Pirate Ship

📦FedEx

Premium Speed

Best for: Express delivery (1-2 day), high-value items, B2B orders

  • Speed: Overnight to 3-day delivery
  • Cost: $15-50+ per package
  • Features: Real-time tracking, delivery confirmation, signature
  • Coverage: Worldwide, strong for US-International
  • Integration: Shopify Shipping, native FedEx account, APIs

📬UPS

Bulk & B2B

Best for: Heavy/bulk orders, B2B fulfillment, regional delivery

  • Speed: Ground (5-7 days), Next Day Air available
  • Cost: Competitive for 1-5 lb packages, discounts for volume
  • Features: Full tracking, COD options, return labels
  • Coverage: Worldwide, strong regional network
  • Integration: Shopify Shipping, UPS Business account

🎁Shopify Shipping

Integrated Rates

Best for: Discounted rates, easy label printing, automatic tracking

  • Carriers: USPS, UPS, FedEx discounted rates
  • Integration: Print labels directly from Shopify Orders page
  • Rates: 15-25% discount vs carrier retail
  • Note: US shipping only (as of 2026)
Carrier Speed Cost Best For Integration
USPS 2-7 days $2.50-8.50 Light, domestic Shopify Shipping
FedEx Overnight-3d $15-50+ Express, B2B Native API
UPS 5-7 days $8-25 Heavy, bulk Shopify Shipping

📫DHL Express

Best for EU

Best for: Cross-border EU, international shipments, customs pre-clearance

  • Speed: 1-3 day delivery throughout EU + UK
  • Cost: €8-30 per package (higher but includes customs)
  • Features: Customs handling included, tracking, signature
  • Coverage: 220+ countries, strong EU network
  • Note: Handles DDP duties automatically

📬DPD (Dynamic Parcel Distribution)

Western Europe

Best for: Western European markets, next-day delivery, competitive pricing

  • Speed: Next-day to 2-day in most countries
  • Cost: €6-20 per package
  • Features: Parcelshop returns, flexible delivery, tracking
  • Coverage: Strong in UK, Germany, France, Benelux
  • Note: Weak in Eastern Europe

✉️Royal Mail

UK/Ireland

Best for: UK domestic, light packages, cost-effective

  • Speed: 1-3 day delivery (Special Delivery, Tracked)
  • Cost: £2.80-10 per package
  • Features: Signature, tracking, compensation
  • Limitation: UK & Ireland only

📦GLS (General Logistics Systems)

Central/East EU

Best for: Central/Eastern Europe, Poland, Czech Republic, Hungary

  • Speed: 2-4 day delivery
  • Cost: €7-22 per package
  • Features: Tracking, parcelshop network, return labels
  • Coverage: 40+ countries, strong in Eastern Europe

📮PostNord

Nordic Region

Best for: Scandinavia (Sweden, Norway, Denmark), Finland

  • Speed: 2-3 day delivery
  • Cost: SEK 50-150 (€5-15)
  • Features: Strong parcelshop network, return service
  • Coverage: Nordic region focus

🚚Yamato Transport (Kuroneko)

#1 Choice

Best for: Domestic Japan, next-day delivery, COD support

  • Speed: Next-day delivery to 95%+ of Japan
  • Cost: ¥800-2,500 per package (varies by zone)
  • Features: Cash on delivery (COD), signature, temperature control
  • Market Share: ~40% of Japan market
  • Address Format: Requires postal code first (〒 format)
  • Note: Peak season surcharges during New Year, GW, Obon

📦Sagawa Express

B2B Specialist

Best for: B2B orders, bulk shipments, corporate accounts

  • Speed: Next-day to 2-day delivery
  • Cost: ¥750-2,200 per package (volume discounts available)
  • Features: Bulk rate discounts, signature, COD available
  • Market Share: ~30% of Japan market
  • Best for: High-volume corporate accounts

✉️Japan Post

Budget Option

Best for: Light items, international shipping, cost-sensitive

  • Speed: 2-5 day domestic, 7-14 day international
  • Cost: ¥500-1,500 domestic (cheapest option)
  • Features: Tracking, signature available, international reach
  • Market Share: ~20% of Japan market
  • Note: Slower but good for economy shipments

🗺️ Shipping Zones & Rates Configuration

Zone Structure

Organize shipping rates by geographical zones and product type:

Zone Definitions

  • Domestic Zone: US → US, EU → EU, JP → JP
  • Regional Zone: Neighboring regions (US-Canada, EU-UK, JP-Asia)
  • International Zone: All other markets (typically federated by carrier)

Product-Based Shipping Profiles

Product Type Weight Range Packaging Carriers
Standard <1 lb (450g) Box, padded USPS, DHL eCommerce, Japan Post
Fragile 1-5 lb (2.3kg) Box, bubble, extra padding FedEx, DHL Express, Yamato
Oversized >5 lb (2.3kg) Flat-rate, pallet-ready UPS, Sagawa, DHL
Temperature <2 lb (1kg) Insulated, cooling packs FedEx Overnight, Yamato cool

Free Shipping Thresholds

Drive order value with free shipping incentives:

  • US: Free shipping on orders $75+ (covers USPS/UPS ground costs)
  • EU: Free shipping on orders €100+ (covers DHL/DPD standard rates)
  • Japan: Free shipping on orders ¥10,000+ (covers Yamato next-day rates)
Pro Tip: Use Shopify Shipping Labels to print labels directly from the Orders page. Discounted rates (15-25% off retail) available for US USPS/UPS initially. International label printing via carrier portals or integrations like EasyPost, Pirate Ship, or Stamps.com.

Rate Configuration Strategy

Shopify supports four rate types:

  • Free: Orders above threshold (e.g., $75+)
  • Flat Rate: Fixed cost per order (simple, good for B2B)
  • Calculated: Carrier API (dynamic, most accurate but requires API setup)
  • Weight-Based: Sliding scale based on package weight

Recommendation: Use Flat Rate for launch (easier to manage), transition to Calculated rates as volume grows and carrier APIs are integrated.

💰 Duties, Customs & Import Taxes

Delivery Terms: DDP vs DDU

Term Definition Who Pays? Customer Experience Recommendation
DDP Delivered Duty Paid Seller (Unicity) Excellent — no surprises ✓ Use
DDU Delivered Duty Unpaid Customer at delivery Poor — unexpected charges ✗ Avoid
Customer Experience Impact: DDU (customer pays duties) leads to cart abandonment and chargebacks. DDP (seller pays) improves conversion and reduces chargebacks. Always use DDP for international orders.

HS Codes (Harmonized System)

Classify all Unicity products with 10-digit HS codes for customs:

  • Supplements/Vitamins: HS 3004 (pharmaceutical), HS 2106 (food preparations)
  • Skincare/Cosmetics: HS 3304 (cosmetics, makeup), HS 3307 (toiletries)
  • Books/Education: HS 4901, 4902 (books, educational materials)
  • Apparel: HS 6100-6500 (various garment types)

Action: Work with compliance team to map all SKUs to HS codes. Input into Shopify product database for customs declaration.

De Minimis Thresholds (Duty-Free Entry)

Packages below these values enter duty-free:

  • US: $800 (most packages qualify)
  • EU: €150 (many Unicity orders exceed this)
  • Japan: ¥16,666 (~$110)

Note: Most typical Unicity orders (US $40-100) stay under thresholds, minimizing duty exposure.

Shopify Duties Estimation

Shopify Markets can calculate and display estimated duties at checkout:

  • Enabled via Shopify Markets (EU, CA, US territories)
  • Requires HS codes on all products
  • Shows as separate line item at checkout
  • Improves transparency and reduces cart shock
Setup Steps:
  1. Assign HS codes to all products in Shopify admin
  2. Enable Shopify Markets for target regions
  3. Configure DDP delivery (Shopify calculates duty cost)
  4. Duties displayed at checkout (customer sees total before paying)

⚙️ Fulfillment Workflow

Step-by-step order-to-delivery process:

  1. Order Placed: Customer completes checkout on Shopify store. Payment captured, order confirmed via email.
  2. Fulfillment Order Created: Shopify creates fulfillment order, assigns to correct warehouse based on shipping country (fulfillment routing rules).
  3. Warehouse Notification: Fulfillment app alerts warehouse team. Order appears in queue for picking.
  4. Pick & Pack: Warehouse staff pick items from inventory, pack securely with packing slip, return instructions, and product docs.
  5. Label Generation: Warehouse prints shipping label via Shopify Shipping (US) or carrier portal (EU, Japan). Label applied to package.
  6. Ship & Track: Package handed to carrier. Tracking number recorded in Shopify order.
  7. Tracking Email: Shopify automatically sends tracking email to customer with carrier link and expected delivery date.
  8. In Transit: Carrier moves package through network. Customer can track real-time via carrier website.
  9. Delivery Confirmed: Carrier marks as delivered, Shopify order status updates to "Fulfilled".
  10. Webhook Notification: Shopify webhook notifies ERP/accounting system (inventory adjustment, revenue recognition).
Shopify Fulfillment App: Mobile app for warehouse staff to view orders, verify items, print labels, and mark shipments. Reduces paper, speeds picking, improves accuracy.

Fulfillment Routing Rules

Shopify automatically routes orders to the correct location based on:

  • Shipping Country: US order → US warehouse, EU → EU warehouse, JP → JP warehouse
  • Stock Availability: If primary warehouse out of stock, route to secondary
  • Distance: Route to nearest warehouse for fastest delivery
  • Manual Override: Admins can manually assign orders to specific locations

🔄 Returns & Exchanges

Return Policy by Market

Market Window Legal Basis Return Shipping Restocking
US 30 days Company policy Free return label No restocking fee
EU 14 days Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU Free returns required No fee (consumer law)
Japan 8 days Act on Specified Commercial Transactions Customer pays (standard) N/A (customer expense)
Legal Compliance: EU 14-day cooling-off period is MANDATORY by law—cannot be shortened. Japan 8-day period applies to distance sales only. Non-compliance results in regulatory fines.

Return Process

Step-by-step return workflow:

  1. Return Request: Customer initiates return via Shopify self-serve portal or contacts customer service. Provides reason (defect, wrong size, changed mind, etc.).
  2. Authorization: System auto-approves (if within policy window) or manual review for edge cases. Return authorization number issued.
  3. Return Label: Shopify generates return label (Shopify Shipping for US, carrier portals for EU/Japan). Sent to customer via email.
  4. Return Shipment: Customer packages item, prints label, ships back to warehouse. Tracking provided.
  5. Warehouse Receipt: Warehouse receives return, scans barcode, inspects condition, verifies contents match order.
  6. Condition Assessment: Item evaluated: Resellable (minor/no damage), Unsellable (major damage, defect), or Partial (missing parts).
  7. Refund Processing: Shopify processes refund to original payment method. Timing varies (3-7 business days depending on bank).
  8. Inventory Adjustment: Item restocked (if resellable) or discarded/liquidated. Inventory count updated in real-time.
  9. Customer Notification: Confirmation email sent when refund processed. Returns tracked by reason for analytics.

Shopify Returns Features

  • Self-Serve Portal: Customers request returns without contacting CS (reduces support load)
  • Return Reasons: Track why items are returned (defect, not as described, wrong size, changed mind) for product feedback
  • Restocking Fees: Configurable percentage charge for used items (recommend 0% for brand loyalty)
  • Exchange Orders: System auto-creates new order when return approved, customer ships item back and receives replacement
  • Return Label Generation: Shopify Shipping (US), carrier portals (EU/JP), or third-party integrations (EasyPost, Pirate Ship)

International Return Logistics

Return shipping is more complex internationally:

  • EU: DHL/DPD return labels included in shipment. Customer scans label, drops at parcelshop. Unicity covers cost (legal requirement).
  • Japan: Customer pays return shipping (standard practice, ~¥1,000-2,000). Provides return address, customer arranges pickup with Yamato/Sagawa.
  • US: FedEx/UPS return labels printed, customer scans barcode at location or requests pickup. Unicity covers cost.
Pro Tip: Include prepaid return label in original shipment for all markets. Increases return rate but improves customer experience and reduces support inquiries. Return cost (~$3-15) justified by retained customer loyalty.

🏪 Multi-Location Fulfillment

Shopify natively supports fulfillment from multiple warehouse locations:

Location Setup

  • Create location per warehouse in Shopify admin (US Warehouse, EU Warehouse, Japan Warehouse)
  • Assign inventory quantities per location
  • Configure fulfillment service (manual vs. automated 3PL)
  • Set fulfillment service cost (if using 3PL)

Fulfillment Priority Rules

Shopify routes orders automatically using:

  • Shipping Country Match: Highest priority. If customer is in US, route to US warehouse.
  • Inventory Availability: Route to location with most stock of needed items
  • Manual Assignment: Admin can override and assign to specific location
  • Priority Ranking: Set preferred order (e.g., US warehouse first, then EU, then Japan)

Split Shipments

When order items are in different locations, Shopify auto-creates separate fulfillments:

  • Order contains items from US + EU warehouse → Two shipments created
  • Each shipment tracked separately, customer receives multiple tracking emails
  • Improves delivery speed (each item ships from nearest location)
  • Higher shipping cost (two labels vs. one) but better customer experience

POS (Point of Sale) Locations

Physical retail stores can also fulfill online orders (Ship-from-Store):

  • Enable on-hand inventory at retail locations
  • Store staff pick, pack, and ship orders using Shopify app
  • Reduces fulfillment center strain during peak seasons
  • Improves delivery speed for local customers
Advanced Strategy: As Unicity expands, implement Ship-from-Store at key retail partners (e.g., GNC stores in US, pharmacy chains in EU) to leverage existing distribution network and improve same-day/next-day delivery capabilities.

📦 Packaging & Labeling Requirements

Market-Specific Requirements

🇺🇸 United States

  • Labels: Standard carrier labels (USPS, FedEx, UPS). Barcodes must be readable.
  • Special Markings: Fragile, Handle with Care, Keep Dry if applicable
  • Packing Slip: Include order details, customer name, return instructions
  • Product Info: Include ingredient/nutrition labels if required (supplements)
  • Regulatory: No special markings required beyond carrier requirements

🇪🇺 European Union

  • Labels: DHL, DPD, or Royal Mail labels with full EU addressing
  • Customs Declaration: CN22/CN23 form (commercial invoice) for international shipments. Shopify can auto-generate.
  • CE Marking: If products require CE certification (certain electronics, supplements under specific regulations), must be on packaging
  • Multilingual Labels: Consider product labels in major EU languages (English, German, French, Spanish) for convenience
  • Recycling Symbols: DSD (Duales System Deutschland) marking required for Germany. Green Dot symbol for packaging.
  • Product Info: Allergen information, expiration date (if applicable), ingredient list (EU law)
  • Return Address: Include warehouse return address clearly on packaging

🇯🇵 Japan

  • Address Format: CRITICAL: Postal code (〒), Prefecture, City, Ward, Address, Building. Yamato/Sagawa require exact Japanese formatting.
  • Product Labels: Japanese product labels REQUIRED for cosmetics, supplements, food items. Include ingredient lists, safety warnings in Japanese.
  • Tracking Number: Label must have 10-digit tracking barcode (Yamato, Sagawa, JP Post format)
  • Fragile Marking: 「壊れやすい」(kowareyasui) for fragile items
  • Express Marking: 「翌日配達」(yokujobi haisou) for next-day delivery, if applicable
  • Contents Declaration: Declare contents clearly (品名: shinmei) with item category (supplement, cosmetic, apparel)

Universal Best Practices

  • Packing Slip: Include in every shipment (customer reference, return instructions, warranty info)
  • Return Instructions: Clear, multilingual instructions on how to return if needed
  • Product Documentation: User guides, warranty cards, care instructions if applicable
  • Branded Packaging: Consider custom inserts/thank you cards for brand loyalty
  • QR Code: Link to tracking page, product reviews, or loyalty program signup
