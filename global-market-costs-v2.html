2. Cross-Border / International Card Surcharge

+1.0% applies when the customer's card is issued in a different region than the store entity.

Exception: EU/EEA cards used in EU/EEA stores are NOT cross-border. This is the biggest cost advantage of having an EU entity. A French card used in a German store = domestic. But a UK card in a German store = cross-border (post-Brexit).

Same-country rule: If card country = store country, always domestic regardless of region.

3. Currency Conversion Fee (FX)

+1.5% applies when the payment currency differs from the payout currency.

Example: Customer pays in GBP but your store settles in EUR. Shopify converts and charges 1.5%. If customer pays EUR and you settle EUR = no FX fee.

4. Multi-Currency Payout (MCP) Fee

+1.5% applies when your payout currency is NOT a domestic currency for your store country.

Dual domestic exemption: Some countries have two domestic currencies. Hungary accepts both EUR and HUF as domestic. Canada accepts both CAD and USD. If your payout is one of these, no MCP fee.

Important: MCP is separate from FX conversion. You can owe FX without MCP (dual domestic scenario) or MCP without FX (settling in non-domestic currency that matches payment currency).

5. Shopify Plus Platform Fee

$2,500/month base platform fee (applies once, not per-transaction). Variable processing fees of 0.40% online and 0.25% POS apply above $625K/month threshold. Expansion stores (up to 9 included with Plus) can each activate local Shopify Payments.
