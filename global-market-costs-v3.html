EU Card Treatment: Under PSD2/interchange regulation, any EU/EEA-issued card is treated as domestic within any EU store. A French Visa in a German Shopify store = no cross-border surcharge. Post-Brexit, UK cards are cross-border in EU stores and vice versa.

Dual Domestic: Some countries allow multiple domestic payout currencies. Hungary confirms EUR + HUF. Canada confirms CAD + USD.

2. Cross-Border / International Card Surcharge

+1.0% when customer card is from a different region than store entity.

Exception: EU/EEA cards in EU/EEA stores are NOT cross-border. Post-Brexit, UK/EU transactions are cross-border.

3. Currency Conversion Fee (FX)

+1.5% when payment currency differs from payout currency.

4. Multi-Currency Payout (MCP) Fee

+1.5% when payout currency is NOT domestic for your store country. Dual domestic exemption: Hungary (EUR+HUF), Canada (CAD+USD).

5. Shopify Plus Platform Fee

$2,500/month base. Variable fees of 0.40% online / 0.25% POS above $625K/month threshold.
