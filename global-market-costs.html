Key Insights

EU Interchange Regulation: EU caps interchange at 0.30% credit / 0.20% debit, making EU Plus rates significantly lower than US/UK.
European Card Treatment: Post-regulation, cards from ANY EU/EEA country are treated as domestic within any EU store. A French Visa used in a German Shopify store = domestic rate.
Post-Brexit: UK-EU transactions are now cross-border. UK card in EU store = international surcharge (+1.0%). EU card in UK store = international surcharge.
Dual Domestic: Some countries (e.g., Hungary) treat both EUR and local currency (HUF) as domestic for payouts, meaning no Multi-Currency Payout fee on either.

The Three Settlement Strategies for Non-EUR EU Countries

Strategy 1: Sell + Settle Local
Customer pays HUF → you get HUF
Rate: ~1.3% + local fixed
FX fee: None
MCP fee: None
Requires: Local bank account in HUF
Downside: You hold local currency, need to manage FX yourself when repatriating
Strategy 2: Sell Local, Settle EUR
Customer pays HUF → Shopify converts → you get EUR
Rate: ~1.3% + local fixed
FX fee: +1.5%
MCP fee: Waived if EUR is domestic (Hungary confirmed)
Requires: EUR bank account, entity in dual-domestic country
Downside: 1.5% FX on every non-EUR transaction
Strategy 3: Sell EUR, Settle EUR
Customer pays EUR → you get EUR
Rate: ~1.3% + €0.30
FX fee: None (same currency)
MCP fee: None
Requires: EUR bank account, price in EUR
Downside: Customer sees foreign currency (unless in Eurozone). Customer's bank may charge them FX
