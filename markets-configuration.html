Shopify Markets Configuration

Multi-currency, multi-language, and regional pricing configuration for selling across US, Europe, and Japan.

▶

What Is Shopify Markets

Shopify Markets allows you to manage international selling from a single store without creating separate storefronts. Each "market" is a group of countries or regions with shared settings for:

  • Currency - USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, etc.
  • Language - English, German, French, Japanese, etc.
  • Domain - Subdomains, subfolders, or country TLDs
  • Pricing - Regional pricing strategies
  • Duties & Taxes - Local tax and duty rules
  • Shipping - Regional shipping zones and rates
Key Feature: Shopify Markets supports up to 50 markets per store, making it ideal for global expansion while maintaining a unified product catalog and inventory system.
▶

Markets vs Expansion Stores

Understanding when to use Markets vs creating fully independent Expansion Stores is critical for architecture decisions.

Shopify Markets

  • Lightweight configuration
  • Same store, different regional settings
  • Unified inventory
  • Single admin dashboard
  • Faster to set up
  • Lower operational overhead

Best for: Similar product catalogs, centralized operations, regional pricing variations

Expansion Stores

  • Fully independent stores
  • Separate product catalogs
  • Separate inventory systems
  • Separate admin dashboards
  • More control & flexibility
  • Higher operational complexity

Best for: Completely different product lines, separate teams, strict regulatory requirements

Recommendation for Unicity International: Use Expansion Stores for major geographic regions (e.g., US Store, EU Store, Japan Store) due to distinct product lines, regulatory requirements, and operational independence. Within each expansion store, use Shopify Markets for sub-regional targeting (e.g., EU store with markets for UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands).

Domain Strategy

Choose a domain structure that balances SEO, user experience, and operational simplicity.

Option A: Subdomains

us.unicity.com eu.unicity.com jp.unicity.com

Pros: Separate DNS and SSL certs, easier multi-store management, clear regional separation

Cons: Potential SEO penalty if not properly configured with hreflang

Best for: Expansion Stores (US, EU, Japan)

Option B: Subfolders

unicity.com/en-us/ unicity.com/en-eu/ unicity.com/en-gb/ unicity.com/de/ unicity.com/fr/ unicity.com/ja/

Pros: Single domain, simpler SEO, shared domain authority

Cons: All content on one domain, more complex URL structure

Best for: Shopify Markets within a single store

Option C: Country TLDs (ccTLDs)

unicity.com → United States unicity.co.uk → United Kingdom unicity.de → Germany unicity.fr → France unicity.it → Italy unicity.es → Spain unicity.nl → Netherlands unicity.jp → Japan

Pros: Strongest local SEO signal, trusts from local search engines

Cons: Multiple domain registrations, higher maintenance complexity

Best for: Enterprise with dedicated regional teams

Unicity International Recommendation:
  • Expansion Stores: Use subdomains (us.unicity.com, eu.unicity.com, jp.unicity.com)
  • Markets within EU Store: Use subfolders (eu.unicity.com/en-gb/, eu.unicity.com/de/, etc.)
  • Future Growth: Consider country TLDs if regional legal entities are established

Regional Pricing Strategy

Implement pricing that reflects regional market conditions, shipping costs, and purchasing power.

Pricing Configuration Methods

1. Fixed Prices per Market

Manually set specific prices for each market.

Product: Immunity Booster ├─ USD: $49.99 ├─ GBP: £44.99 └─ JPY: ¥5,500

Best for stable, predictable pricing with manual control

2. Percentage Adjustments

Apply % markup/markdown to base price.

Base: $50 ├─ US: $50 (0%) ├─ EU: +3% shipping └─ JP: +5% shipping = Auto-calculated

Best for dynamic pricing tied to costs

3. Product-Level Overrides

Set custom prices for specific products in specific markets.

Pro Product (normally +10%): ├─ Japan: +15% (premium market) ├─ EU: +3% (standard) └─ US: 0% (base)

Best for strategic pricing exceptions

Catalog Visibility & Segmentation

Control which products are available in which markets:

  • Show/Hide Products: Hide products that don't meet regional regulatory requirements
  • Variants per Market: Offer different sizes, colors, or options per region
  • B2B Price Lists: Create separate price lists for wholesale partners vs consumer markets
  • Seasonal Availability: Adjust product availability by market and season

Pricing Best Practices

Pricing Psychology:
  • Use "charm pricing" ($X.99) in US, UK, Germany
  • Use "round pricing" (€100) in France, Italy, Spain
  • Avoid 9 endings in Japan (unlucky number); use 0 or 8
  • Consider VAT-inclusive pricing in EU (standard practice)
  • Display all-in pricing (no hidden fees) at checkout

Configure regional tax and duty rules to ensure accurate checkout pricing and compliance.

United States Sales Tax 0-10% (state/local) Calculated at checkout (varies by state)

Customs Duty 5-30% (product-dependent) DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) at checkout

Shopify Markets Tax Configuration

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)

Seller pays all duties/taxes upfront. Displayed price is all-inclusive.

Best for: Japan, premium brands, transparent pricing preference

DDU (Delivered Duty Unpaid)

Customer pays duties/taxes at import. Price shown is before duties.

Best for: EU B2B, cost-conscious pricing

SEO & Hreflang Tags

Properly configure international SEO to ensure Google indexes each market's content correctly.

Hreflang Configuration

Shopify automatically generates hreflang tags for each market and language variant. Example:

<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-US" href="https://us.unicity.com/products/immunity-booster" /> <link rel="alternate" hreflang="en-GB" href="https://eu.unicity.com/en-gb/products/immunity-booster" /> <link rel="alternate" hreflang="de" href="https://eu.unicity.com/de/products/immunity-booster" /> <link rel="alternate" hreflang="fr" href="https://eu.unicity.com/fr/products/immunity-booster" /> <link rel="alternate" hreflang="ja" href="https://jp.unicity.com/ja/products/immunity-booster" /> <link rel="canonical" href="https://us.unicity.com/products/immunity-booster" />

Hreflang Best Practices:
  • Hreflang must be bidirectional (if A links to B, B must link back to A)
  • Include self-referential hreflang (en-US page links to itself)
  • Avoid hreflang errors in Google Search Console (under "International Targeting")
  • Test with Google's URL Inspection tool to verify hreflang is correct
