Option A: Full Shopify Payments

Use Shopify Payments as your sole payment processor. Enables all Shop Pay features and native subscription support. This is the cleanest architecture for subscription-heavy businesses.

  • Full Shop Pay functionality (50% conversion lift)
  • Shop Pay Installments (BNPL)
  • Shop app order tracking
  • Native subscription support

  • No Shopify transaction fees

CURRENT STATE

Option B: Worldpay Only (No Shop Pay)

Keep Worldpay as your payment processor. You retain your negotiated rates but lose access to Shop Pay and native Shopify subscriptions. Subscriptions must be processed through your existing batch system.

✓ Advantages

  • Keep existing Worldpay relationship
  • Negotiated 2.65% rate preserved
  • Existing integration, familiar processes
  • Worldpay handles Apple/Google Pay
  • Full control over card vaulting
  • No migration effort

✗ Disadvantages

  • NO Shop Pay (lose 50% conversion lift)
  • NO native Shopify subscriptions
  • Shopify charges 0.5-2% transaction fee
  • No Shop app tracking
  • Must maintain separate subscription system
  • No Shop Pay Installments
Key Point: Worldpay is NOT on Shopify's approved subscription gateway list. Your 40K monthly autoships must continue through your existing internal system, not Shopify's native subscription features.
THEORETICAL

Option C: Hybrid Model (Shop Pay + Worldpay)

Attempt to run both Shop Pay (via Shopify Payments) and Worldpay simultaneously. This is technically possible in some regions but creates significant complexity and may not support subscriptions properly.
