International Tax Compliance

US sales tax, EU VAT, and Japan consumption tax configuration and compliance for multi-market Shopify operations.

This guide provides configuration guidance only. Consult your tax advisor for compliance decisions.

Tax Landscape Overview

Unicity operates across three major tax jurisdictions, each with fundamentally different tax structures and compliance requirements:

🇺🇸 United States

Sales Tax (State/Local, Destination-Based)

Physical or economic nexus triggers registration requirement. 12,000+ jurisdictions with varying rates.

🇪🇺 European Union

VAT (Value-Added Tax, Included in Price)

VAT at each production stage. Reverse charge for B2B. Rates 17-25% by country.

🇯🇵 Japan

Consumption Tax (10%, Included in Price)

Standard 10% rate. Qualified invoice system required. Tax-inclusive pricing mandatory.

United States Sales Tax

Nexus Requirements

Your business must collect and remit sales tax in a state if you have:

  • Physical Presence: Office, warehouse, employee, or representative in the state
  • Economic Nexus: $100,000+ in sales OR 200+ transactions in a state (varies by state, as low as $0 in some)
Note: As of 2024, most states have adopted some form of economic nexus. You likely have filing obligations even without a physical presence.

Tax Complexity: The 12,000+ Jurisdictions Challenge

The US has approximately 12,000 different tax jurisdictions. Rates vary by:

  • State (7 states have no sales tax: Montana, New Hampshire, Delaware, Oregon, Alaska, Hawaii, and Wyoming)
  • County (within same state, 2-10% variation common)
  • City/Municipal district
  • Special districts (school, transportation, etc.)

Product Taxability

Not all products are taxable. Common exemptions include:

  • Food & Groceries: Generally exempt, but prepared foods and some items vary
  • Supplements/Vitamins: Highly variable by state - some exempt as food, others tax as supplements
  • Medical Devices: Often exempt (prescriptions universally exempt, OTC varies)
  • Clothing: Some states exempt clothing under $110
  • Digital Goods: Most states require sales tax
Unicity Supplements Note: Classification of supplements as "food" vs "taxable product" varies significantly. Document your state-by-state classification and keep records of tax calculations.

Compliance Steps

  1. Identify Nexus States: Review your sales data for states exceeding thresholds
  2. Register: File sales tax permits in each nexus state
  3. Calculate & Collect: Use Shopify Basic Tax or TaxJar/Avalara integration
  4. File Returns: Monthly/Quarterly/Annually depending on state requirements and volume
  5. Remit Taxes: Send collected tax to state revenue departments

Recommended Tools

Shopify Approach: Built-in basic tax calculation exists, but for Unicity's multi-state complexity and supplement classification issues, we recommend:
  • TaxJar - Native Shopify integration, handles 12,000+ jurisdictions, real-time rates
  • Avalara - Enterprise solution, highest accuracy, better B2B handling

European Union VAT

How VAT Works

Value-Added Tax (VAT) is fundamentally different from US sales tax:

  • Tax is collected at each stage of production/distribution
  • Each party charges VAT but recovers VAT paid on inputs (no tax-on-tax)
  • Final consumer bears the total cost - prices displayed must include VAT
  • B2B transactions are often zero-rated (reverse charge mechanism)
Critical: In the EU, displaying prices WITHOUT VAT included is illegal for B2C sales. All consumer-facing prices must show the final, inclusive amount.

Reduced Rates & Product Classification

Supplements and food products may qualify for reduced VAT rates in some countries:

  • Germany: 7% on most food (not beverages), may apply to some vitamins
  • France: 5.5% reduced rate on certain food, 2.1% on daily newspapers
  • Italy/Spain: Various reduced rates for food and health products
  • UK: 0% VAT on most food items (post-Brexit rules apply)
Classification Risk: Supplements fall into gray areas. Determine with local tax advisor whether they classify as food (reduced) or supplementary products (standard). Keep documentation.

B2B Reverse Charge Mechanism

When selling to another business in the EU:

  • Customer must provide a valid VAT registration number (EU format: country code + number)
  • You charge 0% VAT on the invoice
  • Buyer self-assesses and recovers VAT via their VAT return
  • Always validate VAT ID before applying reverse charge (use VIES database)

OSS vs IOSS: Simplification Systems

One-Stop Shop (OSS) for EU-to-EU B2C sales:

  • Single VAT registration covers all EU countries
  • Register in ONE EU country (typically country of establishment)
  • File single quarterly return for all EU sales
  • Threshold: Applies if your cross-border EU sales are under €10,000/year

Import One-Stop Shop (IOSS) for goods shipped from outside EU:

  • Simplifies import VAT for parcels under €150 value
  • VAT collected at point of sale (vs. at customs)
  • Reduces delays and customs handling
  • Required if shipping goods from US to EU consumers

    • Price Display Requirements

    Mandatory:
    • All prices shown to EU consumers MUST include VAT
    • VAT amount should be clearly indicated (separate line: "VAT: €20")
    • Cannot show "plus VAT" or "VAT excluded" to consumers
    • B2B invoices can show VAT separately for registered businesses

Japan Consumption Tax

Tax Rate & Application

  • Standard Rate: 10% on most goods and services
  • Reduced Rate: 8% on food and beverages (prepared foods are 10%)
  • Price Display: Tax-inclusive price display is mandatory (not optional like EU)
  • Applies to: All sales to Japanese consumers and registered businesses

Qualified Invoice System (Tekizei Inboissu)

Since October 1, 2023, Japan requires a new "qualified invoice" system:

  • Registration Number: Businesses must register with the National Tax Agency and obtain a registration number
  • Invoice Format: All invoices must display registration number, tax amount broken down by rate
  • Buyer Records: B2B buyers require qualified invoices to claim input tax credit
  • Transition Period: Full enforcement phase started October 2023 (previous simplified invoice system phased out)
Impact on Unicity: If you issue invoices to Japanese B2B customers, your invoices must comply with qualified invoice format to allow them to recover tax.

Tax Registration (Consumption Tax Registration)

You must register with the National Tax Agency if:

  • Your taxable sales in Japan exceed ¥10,000,000 (approximately $68,000 USD)
  • You are a non-resident seller selling to Japan consumers (must register regardless of sales threshold)

Non-Resident Seller Requirements

Unicity, if non-resident, must:

  • Register for consumption tax in Japan (Form: Application for Consumption Tax Registration)
  • File quarterly/annual tax returns with NTA
  • Use qualified invoice system for B2B sales
  • Maintain records of all tax-inclusive transactions

Shopify Configuration Setup

US Sales Tax Setup

Step 1: Enable Automatic Tax Calculation

  1. Go to Shopify Admin → Settings → Taxes and duties
  2. Select "United States"
  3. Enable "Charge tax on shipping" (optional but recommended)
  4. Choose calculation method: Shopify Basic Tax OR Third-party integration (TaxJar/Avalara)

Step 2: Register Nexus States

  1. Identify all states where you have nexus (based on sales thresholds)
  2. In tax settings, add each nexus state
  3. For each state, configure:
    • Default state rate
    • County-level overrides (if applicable)
    • Product-specific exemptions

Step 3: Connect TaxJar or Avalara (Recommended)

For Unicity: Given supplement classification complexity and multi-state operations, we recommend TaxJar or Avalara:
  • TaxJar: Shopify native app, $99-299/month, handles 12,000+ jurisdictions, real-time rate updates
  • Avalara: Enterprise integration, custom pricing, superior accuracy for B2B, better exemption handling

Step 4: Configure Product Taxability

  1. For each product variant, tag with taxability code: "Taxable" or "Tax Exempt"
  2. For supplements, research your state requirements and document classification
  3. Create product collections by taxability for easier bulk updates
  4. Set "Charge tax on this product" toggle accordingly

Step 5: Test Tax Calculation

  1. Create test orders shipping to different states
  2. Verify correct tax rates and amounts are calculated
  3. Check that product exemptions apply correctly
  4. Confirm county-level specificity (especially for CA, TX, NY)

EU VAT Setup

Step 1: Enable VAT Collection

  1. Go to Shopify Admin → Settings → Taxes and duties
  2. Select "European Union"
  3. Enable "Show VAT tax included in product prices"

Step 2: Set Tax-Inclusive Pricing

Critical: Ensure all prices displayed to EU customers include VAT. This affects:
  • Product pages
  • Cart totals
  • Invoice display
  1. Enable toggle: "Include tax in product prices"
  2. Verify product prices reflect tax-inclusive amounts
  3. Configure shipping tax (may be 0% in some countries)

Step 3: Register VAT Numbers

  1. Under EU tax settings, add your VAT registration numbers:
    • Country of establishment (primary)
    • Any country where you have a warehouse (physical nexus)
  2. These appear on invoices for compliance

Step 4: Configure VAT Rates by Country

  1. Set standard VAT rate for each country you ship to
  2. Add reduced rates for eligible products (food, supplements if qualified)
  3. Example: Set 19% (Germany standard), 7% (Germany reduced), 10% (Ireland standard), 23% (Ireland reduced)
  4. Verify rates match official EU VAT directives

Step 5: Enable B2B Reverse Charge

  1. In tax settings, enable "B2B reverse charge"
  2. At checkout, request customer VAT ID for B2B sales
  3. Validate VAT ID using VIES database (https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies/)
  4. If valid: Apply 0% VAT; if invalid: Apply standard rate and flag for review

Step 6: Digital Goods VAT Configuration

  1. If selling digital products/services, configure VAT based on customer location (not seller location)
  2. Tag digital products accordingly
  3. Set VAT rates for digital goods (may differ from physical goods in some countries)

Japan Consumption Tax Setup

Step 1: Enable Consumption Tax

  1. Go to Shopify Admin → Settings → Taxes and duties
  2. Select "Japan"
  3. Enable "Charge consumption tax"

Step 2: Set Tax Rate & Tax-Inclusive Pricing

  1. Set standard rate: 10%
  2. Enable "Include tax in product prices" (mandatory for Japan)
  3. All product prices shown must be tax-inclusive

Step 3: Configure Reduced Rate (Optional)

  1. If selling eligible food/beverages, tag with reduced 8% rate
  2. Create product collection "Food Items" with 8% rate override
  3. For Unicity supplements: Confirm whether classified as food (8%) or supplement (10%), document decision

Step 4: Qualified Invoice Configuration

  1. Obtain your Japanese Consumption Tax Registration Number from NTA
  2. Add to Shopify settings and invoice template
  3. Ensure invoices display:
    • Registration number
    • Tax rate breakdown (10% and/or 8%)
    • Tax amount calculated separately
  4. For B2B invoices, follow qualified invoice format

Step 5: Test Orders

  1. Create test orders with Japan shipping address
  2. Verify 10% tax is applied and included in total
  3. Check invoice format includes all qualified invoice elements
  4. Confirm pricing display shows tax-inclusive amounts

General Best Practices

Common Pitfalls to Avoid

Critical Errors

1. Displaying Tax-Exclusive Prices in EU (Illegal)

Showing "€100 + VAT" or "Price before tax" to EU consumers violates EU consumer law. All B2C prices must show the final, tax-inclusive amount.

2. Not Registering in Nexus States (Severe Penalties)

Failure to register for sales tax in states where you meet nexus triggers audit exposure and penalties (back taxes + 25-50% penalties + interest). Many states pursue aggressive enforcement.

3. Charging VAT on B2B Reverse Charge Transactions

If a customer provides a valid VAT ID, you must NOT charge VAT. Charging VAT when reverse charge applies creates double taxation and compliance violations.

4. Misclassifying Supplements (US & EU)

Supplements' taxability varies dramatically. In some US states they're tax-exempt as food, in others fully taxable. In EU, classification affects 5-25% VAT rate. Document your classification and keep records.

5. Not Issuing Qualified Invoices in Japan

Since Oct 2023, B2B invoices must include registration number and tax breakdown. B2B customers cannot claim input tax credit without qualified invoices, damaging customer relationships.

Configuration Errors

  • Not enabling tax-inclusive pricing in EU or Japan (causes price display violations)
  • Setting wrong VAT rates per country (e.g., applying 20% everywhere instead of per-country rates)
  • Leaving US tax at 0% or ignoring local jurisdiction rates (TaxJar/Avalara solves this)
  • Not configuring product-level taxability (all products taxed at same rate = incorrect)
  • Forgetting to add B2B reverse charge logic (customers charged when they shouldn't be)

Compliance Oversights

  • Not tracking thresholds (US economic nexus changes, EU VAT threshold management)
  • Failing to file tax returns on time (penalties accumulate quickly)
  • Not maintaining exemption certificates from B2B customers
  • Missing rate updates (states change rates annually, EU sometimes mid-year)
  • Not preserving transaction records for 3-7 years (audit defense)

Tax Exemptions & Overrides

B2B Customer Tax Exemptions

United States:

  • Require valid resale certificate from customer
  • Each state has different form (varies by state)
  • Store and track for audit purposes
  • Some states allow online filing/verification systems

European Union:

  • Require valid EU VAT ID from customer
  • Verify using VIES database (https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies/)
  • Apply 0% VAT (reverse charge)
  • Document validation and keep records

Japan:

  • For B2B transactions, ensure qualified invoice issued
  • Typically no exemption (10% or 8% still applies), but track B2B sales separately

Product-Level Exemptions

In Shopify, you can flag individual products or collections as tax-exempt:

Product → Edit → Taxes ☐ Charge tax on this product Example product categories: • Groceries (US, some EU, Japan) • Medical devices/prescriptions (varies by state/country) • Certain supplements (varies significantly) • Digital goods (different rules by region)

Manual Tax Rate Overrides

For complex scenarios, Shopify allows per-order tax overrides:

  • Use only when automated calculation cannot handle special circumstances
  • Document reason for override in order notes
  • Flag for review during tax filing
  • Verify against applicable tax law before applying
Recommendation: Overrides are error-prone. Instead, resolve at product level or create customer groups with appropriate tax rules.
